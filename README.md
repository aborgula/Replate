# Replate – Food Sharing Mobile Application

**Replate** is an Android mobile application designed to reduce food waste by enabling users to share surplus food with other people in their local communities.

The application allows individuals to publish listings for available food products and homemade meals that can be shared free of charge or for a small symbolic fee. Other users can browse available offers, reserve selected food packages and arrange their collection.

The project was developed as part of my Bachelor's engineering thesis in Computer Science.

## Key Features

- **User Registration and Authentication** – account creation and login using email and password, phone number or Google account.
- **Food Listings** – browsing available food packages in a list or on an interactive map.
- **Search and Filtering** – filtering offers by category, location, distance and collection date.
- **Creating Listings** – publishing offers with descriptions, photos, categories, prices and collection details.
- **Reservations** – reserving selected food packages and managing reservations.
- **Location Services** – discovering available offers nearby using Google Maps.
- **Ratings and Reviews** – reviewing other users after completing a food collection.
- **Favorite Users** – adding selected users to a favorites list.
- **Notifications** – receiving updates about new listings, reservations and collection times.
- **User Profile** – managing contact information and account settings.
- **Online Payments** – integration with the Stripe payment platform.

## Technologies

## Project Goal

Replate aims to encourage local food sharing, reduce food waste and promote more sustainable food consumption habits. Unlike many existing food-sharing platforms, the application focuses on connecting individuals who want to share surplus food rather than exclusively partnering with restaurants and other food businesses.
